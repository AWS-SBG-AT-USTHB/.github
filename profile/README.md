## 👋 Who We Are

We are the **AWS Student Builder Group at USTHB**, a student-led community bringing together learners and builders interested in cloud computing and modern technology.

Our goal is to help students move beyond theory by exploring AWS, sharing knowledge, collaborating on projects, and developing practical technical skills.

We believe the best way to learn technology is to build with it.

## 🎯 What We Focus On

Our community explores a broad range of technologies and engineering practices.

- ☁️ **Cloud Computing & AWS** — Cloud infrastructure, architecture, deployment, and AWS services.
- 💻 **Software Development** — Web applications, APIs, backend systems, and mobile applications.
- ⚙️ **DevOps & Automation** — Docker, CI/CD, Infrastructure as Code, Linux, and monitoring.
- 🤖 **AI & Emerging Technologies** — Exploring AI applications and their integration with modern systems.
- 🔐 **Cloud Security** — Understanding security fundamentals and building more secure solutions.

## 🛠️ Learn by Building

We encourage members to turn what they learn into practical work.

Our GitHub organization is a space to:

- Collaborate on projects and technical experiments.
- Practice Git, GitHub, and open-source workflows.
- Share learning resources and document solutions.
- Experiment with cloud technologies and deployment pipelines.
- Build projects that demonstrate real technical skills.

Explore our repositories to discover what we're working on.

## 🤝 Contribute & Collaborate

Whether you're exploring your first AWS service or already building applications, there's always something new to learn.

1. Explore our public repositories.
2. Read the project's README and contribution guidelines.
3. Open an issue to suggest an improvement or discuss an idea.
4. Submit a pull request when you're ready to contribute.
5. Share what you learn with the community.

Every contribution, question, and shared idea helps us grow.

## 🌱 Our Vision

To build a community of capable, collaborative, and curious engineers who use technology to solve real problems.

We want to create an environment where students learn from one another, experiment with new ideas, and turn knowledge into practical solutions.
