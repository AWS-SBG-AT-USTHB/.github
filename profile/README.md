
# AWS SBG at USTHB 🚀

**Building. Learning. Innovating. Together.**

Welcome to the GitHub organization of **AWS Student Builder Group at USTHB**, a student community at the University of Science and Technology Houari Boumediene, Algeria.

We bring students together to learn, build real-world projects, and collaborate across software development, cloud computing, and DevOps.

## 🎯 What We Do

- ☁️ **Cloud Computing** — Explore AWS services and build cloud-based solutions.
- 💻 **Software Development** — Develop websites, APIs, and mobile applications.
- ⚙️ **DevOps & Automation** — Work with Docker, CI/CD, Infrastructure as Code, and monitoring.
- 🤝 **Open Source** — Collaborate, share knowledge, and contribute to projects.
- 🧪 **Hands-on Learning** — Turn ideas into practical projects and learn by building.

## 🛠️ Our Projects

This organization is home to projects built by our members, from learning experiments and technical challenges to applications designed to solve real problems.

Explore our repositories to see what we're building.

## 🌱 Get Involved

Whether you're just starting out or already have experience, there's room to learn, contribute, and grow with us.

1. Explore our public repositories.
2. Find a project that interests you.
3. Check its README and contribution guidelines.
4. Open an issue or submit a pull request.
5. Share your ideas and learn with the community.

## 🎓 Our Vision

To build a collaborative technical community where students develop practical skills, share knowledge, and create impactful solutions through software engineering, cloud technologies, and DevOps.
